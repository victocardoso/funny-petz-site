Funny Petz

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Funny Petz is a personal content-publishing tool built and operated by a single individual creator to manage the Funny Petz social media accounts (including on TikTok and YouTube). This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is used.

What this tool does

Funny Petz automates the scheduling and publishing of short pet videos that the creator has already produced. It connects to the creator's own social media accounts through each platform's official API to upload and publish video content on that account's behalf.

What data we access

  • Account authorization: an OAuth access token and refresh token issued by the platform for the creator's own account, used solely to publish video content and to check upload status.
  • Basic profile information: the connected account's display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is authorized.
  • Video content: the video files the creator uploads through the tool, which are sent to the platform for publishing.

This tool does not access, collect, or process data belonging to any user other than the account owner who authorizes it. It is not a public-facing service and does not have end users of its own.

How data is used

Access tokens and video files are used exclusively to publish content to the authorized account and are not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with the relevant platform's own API, as required to publish content.

Data retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored only for as long as needed to operate the publishing tool and are deleted if the creator revokes access via their account settings or requests deletion. Published video files are retained by the creator for their own records.

Security

Credentials are stored in a private, access-controlled environment and are not exposed publicly.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the tool evolves. The date above reflects the latest revision.
